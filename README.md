**Aspend** is a modern, privacy-first expense and income tracker built with Flutter, wrapped in a **Material You** interface that adapts to your system's dynamic color palette. Log a transaction in seconds — by hand, by voice, or automatically from a bank SMS — split shared costs with the people around you, and watch it all resolve into balances and charts that actually make sense. No account, no server, no ads. Just your money, on your device.

---

## 💡 Why Aspend?

Most expense trackers make you choose between convenience and privacy. Aspend doesn't.

| | Typical Tracker | **Aspend** |
|---|---|---|
| 🗄️ **Where your data lives** | Cloud account, tied to a login | 100% on-device, in an encrypted local Hive store |
| ✍️ **Logging a transaction** | Manual entry only | Manual, by **voice**, or **auto-detected** from bank SMS/notifications |
| 👥 **Splitting costs with people** | A separate app entirely | Built-in ledger per person, settle up straight through **UPI** |
| 🎨 **Look & feel** | One fixed palette | **Material You** dynamic color, light/dark, custom accents |
| 🌍 **Language** | English, usually | **10 languages** out of the box |
| 🧭 **Learning the app** | Figure it out yourself | One-time **guided tours** for the gestures that aren't obvious |
